What is Tarot?

XVIII · The Moon & XVII · The Star

A deck of archetypes, not a crystal ball. The cards don't predict your future. They reflect the questions you're already holding.

Tarot began in fifteenth-century Italy as a card game, and was only later taken up for divination and symbolism. The Rider-Waite-Smith deck, published in 1909, was designed by Arthur Edward Waite and painted by Pamela Colman Smith. It was among the first to give every card a scene rather than a pattern, and it's still the deck most readers learn on.

Twenty-two Major Arcana trace a journey from the Fool to the World. Fifty-six Minor Arcana describe the everyday, in four suits that each belong to an element.
