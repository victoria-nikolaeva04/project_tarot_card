The site

What is Arcana Noctis?

Arcana Noctis is a tarot reader and card dictionary built around the Rider-Waite-Smith deck. It is made for reflection, not fortune-telling.

You can draw a three-card spread for the past, the present and the future, read every meaning upright or reversed, or simply browse all seventy-eight cards at your own pace. Everything is designed to feel like a candlelit table: slow, calm and a little bit magical.

The reason

Why Tarot?

The cards don't predict your future. They reflect the questions you're already holding.

Tarot is a language of pictures. A tower, a star, a fool stepping off a cliff: each image gives you something to react to, and that reaction often tells you more than the card itself. Pulling a card is a small ritual that asks you to pause, name what is on your mind, and look at it from a new angle.

That is why I wanted to build a place for it. Not to tell anyone what will happen, but to make that pause easy to find.

The deck

The Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot

First published by the Rider Company in the early twentieth century, the Rider-Waite-Smith deck is the foundation of modern Western tarot. It is the deck most readers learn on, and the one used throughout this site.

I

Arthur E. Waite

Designed the symbolic schemas, alignments and occult frameworks behind the deck.

II

Pamela C. Smith

Painted all 78 designs, and was the first to give every Minor Arcana card a full scene.

III

Public domain

Released over a century ago, which is why its images are free to use and adapt.
